Quintek

Questions written from your own notes, checked before you see them.

Every plan runs the same engine and the same validator. What differs is how many questions you can generate, not how good they are.

What the numbers mean

Monthly and daily are different limits
A plan's monthly allowance is the total for the billing period. The daily cap is how much of it you can use in one day. You can have thousands left for the month and still be finished for today — the app always tells you which one applied.
Asking for more than you have left is not a refusal
Ask for 500 with 173 left and you are offered the 173, with the reason. Nothing is generated before you agree to it.
Rollover
Unused questions carry into the next period up to half of that period's normal allowance. They do not accumulate indefinitely.
Changing plans
An upgrade starts immediately and is prorated. A downgrade starts at your next renewal, so you keep what you have already paid for. Cancelling keeps your access to the end of the period you have paid for; nothing is charged again.
